Help with Cloudz

Get in touch

Something not working, or an idea for Cloudz? Email info@franceschienterprises.com. It helps to say which device you're using and what you tapped before the problem.

My clouds aren't syncing to my other devices

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on each device, and make sure iCloud is allowed for Cloudz: in the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud. When Cloudz can't back up, a banner at the top of the journal says why.

How do I share a cloud?

Open the cloud, tap the share button, choose Invite people… and send the link. The people you invite need Cloudz and an iCloud account. When they open the link, the cloud arrives in their journal.

Why can't I see the other outlines on a shared cloud?

That's the game. On a cloud someone shared with you, the others' outlines stay hidden until you trace what you see. Then they're revealed one by one. Tap Show me to see them without tracing, but then you can't trace that cloud.

I'm not getting notifications

In Cloudz, open Settings and turn on Notifications under Permissions. Also check that notifications are allowed for Cloudz in the Settings app. Notifications don't show while Cloudz is open; the cloud gets a blue dot instead.

How do I stop sharing, or leave a shared cloud?

Open the cloud and tap the share button. If it's your cloud, choose Stop sharing…. You can keep or remove the outlines your friends added. If someone shared it with you, choose Leave this cloud….

How do I delete everything?

In Cloudz, open Settings and tap Delete All Data. This removes every cloud and outline from your device and from iCloud, on all your devices, and can't be undone. Read the privacy policy for more.
