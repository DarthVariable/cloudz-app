Cloudz

Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version

Cloudz doesn't collect anything about you. Your clouds stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account. The developer has no servers and can't see your photos, outlines, voice notes or places. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What Cloudz keeps, and where

  • The photos you add, from the camera or from your photo library.
  • The outlines you trace, with their names, who saw them and their colors.
  • Voice notes, but only the ones you record for an outline.
  • When each photo was taken and, if Location is on, the town where you added it and its coordinates.
  • The names of the people in your journal, and your settings.

All of this is stored on your device and, when you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud database, so it can sync to your other devices. Only you can read it there. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you aren't signed in to iCloud, everything stays on your device.

Sharing a cloud

When you invite people to a cloud, it's shared through iCloud with those people only. They can see that cloud's photo, its outlines with their names and colors, its voice notes, and when and where it was taken. Their outlines come back to you the same way.

You can stop sharing a cloud at any time, and anyone can leave a cloud that was shared with them. Cloudz shows the names iCloud has for the people in a share.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos of clouds.
  • Photos: Cloudz uses Apple's photo picker, so it only gets the photos you choose. It asks to add to your library only when you save a Live Photo.
  • Microphone: to record voice notes.
  • Location, while using the app: to save the town with a new cloud. The town name is looked up through Apple's location services. You can turn this off in Cloudz's Settings.
  • Notifications: to tell you when someone traces, joins or changes a cloud you share. They arrive through iCloud and Apple's notification service, and are worded on your device.

You can change any of these at any time in the Settings app.

What Cloudz doesn't do

  • No accounts and no sign-in, beyond the iCloud account on your device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code.
  • Nothing is sold or passed on, and none of your data reaches the developer.

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send the developer anonymous crash reports and usage figures. You can turn that off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single cloud or outline in Cloudz.
  • Settings > Delete All Data removes every cloud and outline from your device and from iCloud, on all your devices. Clouds you've shared are deleted for everyone, and you leave clouds others shared with you.
  • Stop sharing a cloud, or leave one shared with you, from its share menu.
  • You can also see and remove Cloudz's iCloud data in the Settings app, under your name > iCloud.

Children

Cloudz doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included: nothing you add to Cloudz reaches the developer.
